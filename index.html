The $1 Program

How Baltimore's
$1 Home Program Works

Baltimore City sells vacant, city-owned homes to eligible residents for just $1 — in exchange for a commitment to renovate and live there. Here's the short version:


  • Baltimore City Resident — Current city residents get priority during the homeowner application window
  • Renovation Funds Ready — You'll need to show access to renovation funding (typically $90K+) before city approval
  • Renovate Within 1 Year — The home must be fully renovated within 12 months of purchase — that's exactly where we come in
  • Make It Your Home for 5 Years — The property must serve as your primary residence for at least five years
Program details are subject to change For official eligibility rules and currently available properties, visit the Baltimore City DHCD Fixed Pricing Program page →
